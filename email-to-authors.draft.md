Dear Dr Dunne and Prof Wells,

I have ported Isabelle/HOL/GST to Isabelle2026-RC0. The session builds, all
57 theories are checked, and the document now builds to PDF without LaTeX
errors. My fork is at

  <repository URL>

with a `PORT_LOG.md` recording every change and how it was checked.

Three things in the port may be of interest beyond the mechanical updates.

**Syntax removals had silently stopped working.** Isabelle's mixfix
templates now carry nested-cartouche markup, and a `no_notation` whose
template does not match exactly is ignored without any warning — so every
removal in `remove_syntax.thy` had quietly become a no-op. The visible
symptom was `Soft_Types.thy` failing to parse, because the soft-type
notation `x : P` was ambiguous against `Set.member`'s ASCII `:`. Where the
library offers a declaration bundle I switched to `unbundle no X_syntax`,
which does not depend on template text.

**`ROOT` named only `Founder/Test`,** so any theory nothing imports was
never built, and so had never been checked. Naming the leaves as well
raised the session from 54 theories to all 57. That also surfaced four
files left over from earlier renames; one of them (`Ordinal/Model/mSucc.thy`
alongside `msucc.thy`) made Isabelle refuse the session outright as a
case-insensitivity collision.

**I added the example GSTs from figure 4 of the CICM 2021 paper** — ZF, ZFP,
ZFN and ZFE — each generated by `mk_gst` and with the cover and disjointness
conditions proved. Only ZF+ was present before. Two details I would value
your correction on:

- I read "ZFO" in the repository's `todo` as a slip for **ZFE**, since the
  figure has no ordinal GST.
- The 2022 paper reuses the name ZF+ for a different combination than the
  2021 one, so I kept both: the existing `ZFplus` and a new `ZFplus21`.

Writing those also turned up a typo that nothing had ever referenced:
`GST_Features.thy` declared the ordinal-recursion feature record as
`val OrdReec`.

**My question is about licensing.** The repository carries no `LICENSE`
file, so the terms the work is available under are unstated. I would like
to know what you intend, both for the fork as it stands and for a possible
Archive of Formal Proofs submission — the AFP requires an entry to name a
license and its authors, and neither is mine to choose. If a submission
would be welcome, I am happy to do the remaining mechanical work; if you
would rather submit it yourselves, or not at all, the port is yours to take
either way.

With thanks for the development, and for the papers.
